Meet Elluryen

The stone at her throat

Elluryen walks the in-between, where the forest meets the night sky. Long ago she gathered one stone from every moon of the year and strung their light on a single silver thread.

The blue crystal she never takes off is her own. Each month she holds it and calls the next moon’s stone awake, then shares its chant, its card and its word with everyone born beneath it.

“Carry your light gently. It was given to you on purpose.”
